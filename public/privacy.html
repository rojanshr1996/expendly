Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

🔒 Core Summary: Expendly is designed on the principle of Fiscal Calm. All your personal financial data (income, expenses, budgets, categories) stays 100% strictly on your local device. We do not collect, transmit, or sell your financial information to external cloud servers.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Expendly ("we", "us", or "our") handles user privacy when you use our mobile application ("Expendly" or the "App") and website located at https://expendly.web.app.

We respect your privacy rights and are committed to maintaining the highest level of transparency regarding your financial data.

2. Data Collection and Storage

A. Financial & Transaction Data

All financial records, transaction logs, category tags, amounts, and currency settings created within Expendly are stored locally on your mobile device using encrypted SQLite storage (via Drift database framework).

  • We do not require user registration or account creation.
  • We do not upload your transaction data to cloud databases.
  • We do not perform cloud backup sync unless explicitly initiated by you through your personal device storage.

B. Analytics & Telemetry

Expendly operates with zero telemetry tracking for app usage. We do not use third-party user behavioral tracking services to log your financial activities or app navigation.

3. Advertising & Google AdMob Integration

To support free development and app maintenance, Expendly incorporates Google AdMob services to display advertisements within the application.

A. Google AdMob SDK Data Usage

Google AdMob may collect and process certain non-personally identifiable technical information and device identifiers in accordance with Google's Advertising Policies and applicable privacy laws (such as GDPR and CCPA). This information may include:

  • Device model, operating system version, and system language settings.
  • Advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID on Android or IDFA on iOS).
  • IP address for coarse geographical region determination (country or city level).
  • Ad interaction metrics (impressions, clicks, and ad performance metrics).

B. Personalized vs. Non-Personalized Advertisements

Depending on your region and privacy choices on your device:

  • Personalized Ads: Google AdMob may use device advertising identifiers to show ads relevant to your interests.
  • Non-Personalized Ads: If you opt out of ad personalization or reside in jurisdictions requiring explicit consent, AdMob will serve contextually targeted, non-personalized ads without using advertising tracking profiles.

C. Controlling Ad Preferences

You can manage or reset your device advertising ID at any time via your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset Advertising ID / Delete Advertising ID.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track".

For more details on how Google processes ad data, please review the Google Privacy Policy and Google Advertising Policies.

4. Third-Party Links & Services

Our website or application may contain links to third-party services (such as GitHub or developer contact channels). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Expendly is not intended for or directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal data, we will immediately take steps to remove such data.

6. Your Data Rights

Because all your financial data resides solely on your mobile device:

  • Access & Modification: You can view, edit, or delete any expense or category directly inside the app at any time.
  • Data Erasure: Clearing app data or uninstalling Expendly permanently erases all locally saved financial records from your device.

7. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal regulations or app features. The revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
