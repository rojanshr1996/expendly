Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my financial data stored? +
All transaction logs, budget categories, and currency settings are stored strictly on your local mobile device using Drift SQLite. Nothing is sent to cloud servers.
Does Expendly sell or track my usage? +
No. Expendly operates with zero user telemetry and zero behavioral tracking. AdMob ads served in app adhere to Google's standard ad policies.
How can AdMob crawlers verify this domain? +
The root domain hosts https://expendly.web.app/app-ads.txt which specifies the authorized seller information for Google AdMob crawlers.
