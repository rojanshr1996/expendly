Why Expendly?

Built for People Who Value Privacy & Simplicity

Most finance apps require email sign-ups, bank links, and upload your financial history to cloud servers. Expendly takes a refreshing consumer-first approach.

🔒

100% Offline & Private

All financial entries, budgets, and settings are saved strictly on your smartphone. No cloud accounts, zero tracking, and zero telemetry.

💾

On-Device Storage

Your financial records live exclusively inside your phone's memory. Enjoy instant responsiveness with complete data security.

⚡

No Sign-up Required

Open the app and start logging immediately. No password fatigue, no phone number registration, and no email newsletters.

Calming Design System
